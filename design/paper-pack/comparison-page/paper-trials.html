Round 1 pick

A, with two ideas borrowed from B

A gets most of the look for the least cost: no new library, a 313 KB image, and nothing changes about how the app starts. B looks the most polished, but every web visit waits for a 3 MB download before anything draws, which is a lot for a timer. C needs the most code and its repeating textures show on large sheets.

  • Take B's kraft background. It separates the cream pieces from the page better than A's cream-on-cream.
  • Take B's faint lit top edge. It is a thin highlight path, so SVG can draw it too.
  • Tone down A's fibers a little, and fix its chip ends and dark-mode torn edge.
  • Check shadows and performance on a real Android phone before going further.
